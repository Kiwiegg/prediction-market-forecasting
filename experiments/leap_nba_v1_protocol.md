# LEAP-NBA v1 frozen protocol

Research question: does explicit evidence-level likelihood estimation improve probability accuracy and calibration relative to direct LLM probability elicitation? This protocol and its prompt, group definitions, response schema and configuration must be committed before paid inference. Freeze their hashes in the runner. No outcome-dependent amendments to v1.

## Paper review and adaptations

Reviewed [LEAP, arXiv:2609.01337v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.01337v1), particularly §§3–4 and Appendix B. The paper isolates evidence-level elicitation from deterministic posterior calculation, uses conditional independence as a working assumption, and discusses dependency clustering, reliability sampling, and removal diagnostics. Its discrete elicitation uses qualitative support mappings. Our NBA experiment instead uses four fixed semantic groups, directly elicited numeric binary likelihood ratios, a fixed neutral prior, one draw per available group, and two prespecified tempering coefficients. We do not implement its learned/elicited priors, dependency clustering, reliability sampling, continuous-target safeguards, or agent collection loop. This is an adaptation, not an exact reproduction.

Each group gets game identity, home/away identities/designations, recorded tipoff, cutoff and common reconstruction/verification qualifiers, plus only its own fields. Group definitions are in `leap_nba_v1_groups.json`. Strength includes the existing per-game differential and aggregates, recent form and split records; schedule gets only neutral-site and cutoff rest. Missing feature annotations are restricted to their own group. Player availability includes official report metadata, uncertainty and player statuses, plus already-validated injury/lineup news. Additional news receives only roster/team-development findings, with deterministic normalization/source-claim deduplication. Source references stay with their own findings. No audit/evaluation files or search snippets enter requests.

An empty/unavailable group skips inference and contributes LR=1, with state and reason preserved. Strength with no completed games skips separately from source failure. Venue designation can make schedule usable without rest. Reports with only unavailable/not-submitted/ambiguous information skip; retain all states and never infer health. Official reports require existing publication-verification evidence and eligible publication timestamps. Missing historical verification flags remain explicit, not upgraded. No usable news differs from no news collected. Deduplication uses explicit categories and normalized factual identities; it does not infer semantics beyond existing annotations.

## Fixed elicitation

Default Anthropic `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`, temperature 0, maximum 128 output tokens, structured JSON, no tools/search. `prompts/leap_nba_lr_v1.txt` is the sole fixed prompt. Configurable Anthropic or explicit HTTPS JSON gateway; mocks are always labeled separately. v1 generation settings are fixed across all games. Request identity binds game, parent evidence hash, group hash, prompt, wire schema, provider/model and generation settings. Different alphas never change requests.

Output exactly `{"likelihood_ratio": number, "evidence_sufficiency": category}`. Categories: adequate = usable evidence; limited = usable but incomplete/uncertain; insufficient = unable to estimate. Strictly reject booleans, strings, duplicate/extra keys, nonstandard numbers, invalid/fenced JSON, nonfinite/nonpositive ratios, ratios outside **[0.05,20] inclusive**, malformed categories and insufficient responses. Insufficient is an observed failed inference, including when accompanied by LR=1. Bounds limit a single group to absolute log support <= log(20); they are design safeguards, not statistical calibration. No clipping or retry. Flag accepted LR <=0.1 or >=10 as extreme for diagnosis only. Preserve all raw responses privately, including invalid ones, without credentials. Record prompt/group/parent hashes, settings, timestamps, usage, latency and errors.

Calling these values likelihood ratios does not establish statistically calibrated likelihood ratios. Treat them as LLM estimates. Calls may import remembered team strength or historical outcomes despite instructions; historical experiments are exploratory.

## Aggregation and failure semantics

Prior P(home)=0.5, logit prior=0. For available successful groups let z_i=log(LR_i); legitimate absent groups z_i=0. Compute log_odds=alpha*sum(z_i), p=sigmoid(log_odds), stably. Fixed variants: raw alpha=1.0 and conservative alpha=0.5. Same saved observations for both; do not fit alpha, rank variants as a tuning choice, or make new calls for a variant. Any failed, insufficient, interrupted/uncertain or budget-blocked required group makes both full forecasts missing, not neutral. Preserve successful partial group results. Leave-one-group-out p is sigmoid(alpha*sum over remaining z_i), computed offline only for complete forecasts. Save unweighted log-LRs, alpha-weighted contributions, omission posterior, and full-minus-omission probability deltas.

Within and across groups evidence is dependent: season/recent records and differential overlap, split records already encode venue, and injuries can affect strength. A/B can double-count home advantage. Tempering is a fixed sensitivity analysis, not a proven correction. Identity-only home advantage and unavailable player reports warrant qualitative scrutiny.

## Immutable execution and budgets

Manifest before calls; shared per-request cache with exclusive attempt claim before network. Terminal errors never automatically retry; uncertain claims are never resent, even from a new run directory. Sealed experiments and result/cache files are immutable. Repeated runs reuse matching records; read-only verification must reproduce requests, parsing and aggregation from original captures, with no network or writes. Default five-game scope; full development and reserved evaluation require explicit operator commands. Evaluation additionally requires a separate authorization flag, checked before loading its packets.

This pilot authorizes <=20 new Anthropic requests, <=$1.00 estimated inference cost, no paid retries or search. Reserve an upper bound per call before transmission using payload bytes plus 4096 input tokens and 128 output tokens at conservative $3/$15 per million; stop before exceeding either budget. Actual reported-token cost is an estimate at these configured rates, not an invoice. Record unknown-usage failures at reserved cost. Example configuration is frozen before calls. Later full runs need operator-specified bounds and authorization; never auto-run 50 or 200.

## Evaluation after inference seals

Reuse existing outcome/market validation and proper-score functions. Join only requested development IDs after inference is sealed. Compare saved direct, raw, conservative, Kalshi preferred quote midpoint and constant0.5 on the **same intersection** of valid forecasts, finalized labels and valid preferred quotes. Report exact IDs, counts, per-method availability/failures and exclusion reasons. Brier primary; natural-log loss secondary with epsilon=1e-15 clipping only for log loss; ten equal-width probability buckets with counts. Report paired per-game score differences, group contributions and offline removal sensitivity, reported tokens, latency, estimated cost and cache reuse separately. A direct-vs-market-only summary may have a different denominator and must be labeled. No held-out tuning or scoring. Preserve the existing market rule: two-sided YES quote candle ending <=cutoff and <=1200s lag; candle end does not verify quote-refresh age.

Five games validate implementation and permit qualitative diagnosis only. No confidence, superiority or calibrated-skill claim. Prospective frozen evidence remains the eventual primary out-of-sample test. Unverified historical schedule/source versions, memorization, numeric LR interpretation, evidence dependence and unequal inference budgets remain methodological limitations.
