# Frozen prospective NBA study, Phase 1

Commit this protocol, configuration, asset registry and tested orchestration **before collecting any scored forecast**. The historical 200 games, split, evidence and completed experiments remain unchanged. Direct, LEAP v1 and LEAP v2 retain their original prompts, model (`claude-haiku-4-5-20251001`), generation settings, group definitions, priors, LR bounds/mapping and alpha1/0.5 aggregation. No tuning during this study. Historical results motivated the study but are not pooled with prospective observations.

## Sample and fixed analysis

The eventual study target is **at least 100 primary-eligible games**: the first 100 qualifying fixtures in recorded T−24h chronological order, game ID breaking ties. Enroll scheduled NBA regular-season fixtures without considering outcomes, prices or forecast probabilities. Keep every collection attempt, source failure, method abstention and exclusion. Provider/source availability may make this sample selective; report it. Freeze a chronological enrollment ledger before later outcome joins. No collection campaign, recurring scheduler or automatic continuation is authorized in Phase 1; only one integration pilot is authorized.

Primary metric: **mean paired Brier difference, v2 raw minus Direct**. The primary intersection needs an eligible frozen capture, pre-tipoff sealed records, finalized verified outcome and both forecasts; it does not require v1 or Kalshi. Secondary metrics: natural-log loss (original epsilon1e-15 clipping only for log loss), original ten-bin calibration with counts, v2 conservative, v1 raw/conservative, constant50% and Kalshi. The five-method market intersection adds v2 conservative, Kalshi and constant; the seven-method intersection additionally requires both v1 variants. Always label exact IDs/n, retain missingness and never compare unmatched aggregates as paired results. Prespecify 10,000 paired game-row bootstrap resamples, seed20261023, shared row indices within each cohort and 95% percentile intervals with linear (B−1)q interpolation. Do not optimize prompts, priors, mappings, alpha or thresholds after observing outcomes. No early stopping based on scores or significance. Phase1's one game cannot establish predictive skill.

## Timing and source policy

The target is **exactly 86,400 elapsed seconds before recorded scheduled tipoff**, calculated in UTC, including daylight-saving transitions. Require explicit timezone-aware schedule timestamps. Prefer NBA-corroborated ESPN discovery; retain fallback status and source clocks. Reject completed, canceled, postponed, ambiguous-time and non-regular-season fixtures before research. The first registered game identity, home/away/venue and scheduled tipoff are immutable.

Operational tolerance is **±300 seconds**. Record collection start, actual frozen snapshot, packet creation, each model request/response and final sealing time. Collection start, snapshot and packet creation must all fall within this window. Distinguish exact capture (actual snapshot exactly equals target), early/late within tolerance, and early/late outside tolerance. Never backdate. An operationally eligible capture is not an exact-time forecast. All inference must finish and seal before recorded tipoff; report inference/seal deviation separately. An off-time capture may remain an audit observation but never enters primary/secondary scoring or triggers forecasting.

The **source information cutoff is min(actual snapshot, target T−24h)**. A +3-minute capture cannot use a report/news update or source payload retrieved after target. For pregame statistical history, both completed-game source clocks and actual retrieval must be at/before this cap; unsupported or revised-later records remain missing. Preserve original feature definitions: rest is computed at the actual recorded snapshot from eligible past-game facts; this deterministic clock calculation does not introduce a new source. No post-event target summary is fetched during collection. Past completed-game results are used only to construct the already defined W/L/form/margin/rest features. Raw schedule/summary odds and scores stay audit-only and are not passed to models.

Reuse existing deterministic statistical/injury parsing and bounded Brave/Anthropic news extraction. Undated, future-dated, modified-after-cutoff, late-retrieved, unsupported-domain or unsupported-excerpt news remains unusable under existing validation rules. No search snippets enter evidence/model context. Official PDFs require consistent issue/creation/capture clocks, with actual retrieval treated as an availability upper bound, not first-publication proof. Missing/unsubmitted reports never imply health. Do not retrieve historical sources later to reconstruct a missed live snapshot.

Collection readiness requires both team season schedules to corroborate the fixture and both season records to be reconstructible; a verified zero-game record is legitimate. Other unavailable features, injuries and news remain explicitly missing. Failure to establish the readiness condition freezes/preserves the audit but blocks forecasting. Missing source groups contribute LR1 only under existing semantics; failed or insufficient inference never becomes neutral.

A successful bounded research attempt with no usable articles/claims is valid missing news. A search/provider/extraction error is separately marked `collection_failed`, preserved in the audit and blocks forecasting rather than masquerading as no news found. Access denials abort immediately with a permanent stop journal.

The legacy v2 source-qualifier validator accepts `timestamped_history_unversioned_payloads` and not the older prospective collector's newer label. Newly constructed study packets use that **conservative existing classification for unversioned timestamped source payloads**, with verification flags false. Actual prospective collection mode is explicit in the immutable capture run and `snapshot_timing`; source clocks and lineage remain intact. This is collection-schema compatibility, not changing an original validator, projection, statistical feature, frozen packet, prompt or method. Existing historical/prospective captures are never relabeled or overwritten.

## Shared evidence, inference and budgets

Freeze one consumer-neutral packet and manifest; Direct/v1/v2 load it through the original shared snapshot loader. Direct receives exactly the full packet; each LEAP method uses its unchanged group projections. All records bind the identical parent hash, with group/prompt/request hashes and settings. No raw source audits, target outcomes, odds or prices enter forecast context. No model tools, browsing or retrieval. Raw/conservative variants share one set of group calls each. No new historical inference.

Phase1 global limits cover **research plus forecasting**: at most **20 Anthropic requests**, **two Brave searches**, **four article GET attempts**, **$1 cumulative conservative pre-call reservations**. Research additionally retains the configured smaller budget. Forecast reservations use the original per-method conservative bounds/rates; extraction reserves full request bytes plus overhead and output-token bound. Counts/costs are shared, not reset between stages. Incomplete or unknown-usage attempts retain reserves. No paid retry, automatic resend or deletion of claims. New public/article requests also use one HTTP attempt. Stop all network/paid work on the first HTTP403 or explicit access denial, preserving a permanent stop journal. Do not change endpoints, credentials or output directories to circumvent it. A subsequent operator authorization is required before another live attempt.

The CLI selects only a currently eligible upcoming game. If none can be verified, perform an offline dry run; do not wait automatically, fabricate a fixture or spend outside the window. The one-pilot authorization binds a fixed output/game/protocol; successful captures, caches, partial results and seals remain immutable. Use original shared native caches. Test doubles/mocks are explicitly marked and cannot enter prospective scoring.

## Schedule changes, outcomes and independent market benchmark

Do not change an already frozen game time or re-use its forecast for a new target. Postponed/canceled games are excluded with reason. Any subsequent scheduled-time, home/away/venue identity conflict or unverifiable schedule confirmation excludes strict evaluation; preserve original forecasts. A later authorized study may enroll a newly scheduled fixture at its new cutoff, without overwriting the first record. Do not silently roll a game to a different day or choose a new cutoff retrospectively.

Outcome collection/evaluation is a **separate CLI**, gated by a completed pre-tipoff seal and current time after recorded tipoff. An authoritative ESPN final summary must corroborate game/teams, unchanged scheduled date, final status, timestamped End Game and game-specific source clocks. Reject ongoing games, future outcome timestamps, mismatched teams, unverified final facts and mock captures. Preserve raw final sources separately. Failed forecasts/market absence remain missing, not replaced by 50%; constant50% is a separately labeled comparator.

Capture Kalshi independently with `prospective_evaluate.py --capture-market`, in a different output directory/process. It reads the registered schedule only, never supplies data to the collector/forecasters. Capture live `/milestones` and `/markets/{home-YES-ticker}` responses; require a unique related basketball milestone with agreeing scheduled tipoff and verified home-team strike mapping, an open market, and a valid two-sided dollar quote `0<bid<=ask<1`. Take the current YES bid/ask midpoint; keep raw responses, retrieval times, hashes, spread/quote metadata and identity validation. No trade or retrospective candle substitution. The historical **20-minute candle rule is unchanged**; this prospective benchmark is a live observation.

The quote response must finish **at/before target and no earlier than target−300 seconds**. Record exact observation time, offset and exact-time flag; this is a bounded operational observation, not proof of an instantaneous T−24h quote or of the underlying quote's refresh age. Late (+tolerance) quotes are not accepted as pre-cutoff prices. If cutoff capture fails, the market stays missing even when primary model forecasts are eligible. Do not fetch prices later to backfill. Outcomes and benchmark records are evaluation-only and never model evidence. No paid inference or campaign is run by the evaluator.

## Audit and reproducibility

Store immutable raw sources, provider captures, claims/reservations, errors, timestamps and hashes outside Git; publish sanitized packet/forecast audits and derived results without credentials or private raw provider responses. Private replay verifies captures/source extraction and caches; public replay verifies shared packet identity, frozen request specifications, structured response validation and aggregation, not provider authenticity. No late/source-failed capture is advertised as eligible. Report mocks separately from live validations, cost estimates separately from invoices, usage-unknown reserves separately from known token estimates, and all method/source missingness. Prospective capture alone does not independently verify every publisher's first-publication/version history.
