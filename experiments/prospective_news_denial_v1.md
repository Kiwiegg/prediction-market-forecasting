# Prospective third-party article denial amendment v1

**Proposed; disabled until explicitly adopted.** This operational amendment applies
only to new prospective collection after its recorded effective time. It changes
no model prompt, model, statistical feature, LR mapping, aggregation, scoring or
T−24h information/freeze/sealing deadline. Preserve the original news-v1 cohort,
100-game study and pilot; label subsequent observations with this amendment in
separate source/job audits and report policy strata rather than silently pooling.

An HTTP403 from an allowlisted **third-party dated news article** can be missing
evidence. Save the original response, URL, clocks/hash and permanent denied-host
journal. Never extract claims from it. Do not retry that request, use proxy/auth
workarounds, follow redirects, switch endpoints or retrieve another page from the
denied host. Normalize case, www and default HTTPS ports to avoid host aliases.
Per-host locks prevent a concurrent worker from starting another request after
the first denial becomes known. Already-denied hosts produce an audit-only skip
without invented retrieval timestamps or fabricated article observations. Other
allowed sources can continue under the same fixed query/page/token/cost budgets.
Empty/unavailable news remains explicit; it never implies player health.

This exception does **not** apply to Brave/Anthropic/gateway responses, official
NBA/team sources, HTTP401/407/451, credential challenges, API-key/account/billing
restriction messages, integrity failures or deadline errors. Those retain fatal
request/access-denial behavior and no retries. Unknown/ambiguous403 classification
fails closed. Provider rate/server/malformed-response failures remain failed
requests with retained claims; this policy never retries or turns them into news.
Publication and modification timestamps, verbatim support, domain restrictions,
redirect rejection and leakage validation are unchanged. Search snippets remain
audit-only. Post-cutoff extraction processes only immutable pre-cutoff bytes.

## Preserved journals and reviewed recovery

Do not delete or overwrite original `circuits`, paid claims, stage artifacts,
request identities or campaign approvals. An audited exact-owner release uses the
existing terminated-run confirmation and R2 conditional write, preserving the
checkpoint and creating the original immutable recovery journal. The failed run
must be completed/failure/attempt1 at its exact recorded commit. A fresh audit
must match the operator-provided SHA, current owner digest and checkpoint, verify
all captures/replays/17 responses and establish that all incomplete deadlines have
passed. Any unknown response, changed ledger, extra denial, owner drift, active
partial game or ambiguous account restriction blocks this recovery path.

Releasing ownership does not clear a circuit or activate this amendment. Keep
LIVE_ENABLED=false. Later, explicit operator adoption appends a scope-preserving
campaign amendment and separate immutable policy authorization, linking the
reviewed audit, unchanged ledger, exact original global-circuit body hashes,
policy/operational registry hashes and effective timestamp. Only those two exact
historical `network`/`paid` records attributable to the audited ordinary AP403
can be treated as superseded for future jobs. Their rows/files remain intact.
`host:apnews.com` remains blocked. A new provider/global denial never matches the
old body hashes and still stops all paid work. Do not apply this to another
incident without a separate review. Changing approval or policy invalidates the
linked exception rather than silently broadening it.

The six unfinished enrolled games remain immutable unsuccessful attempts. Four
started collection; two never started. They cannot be frozen, inferred or
backfilled now. Keep their enrollment slots and reservations; the cumulative
30-attempt cap, original expiry and $50/$1.35 ceilings do not reset. Only future
game cutoffs after adoption can acquire new claims. Forecasts/outcomes/markets
are never selection inputs. The prior completed pilot is replayed, not re-elicited.

The operational migration preserves both earlier registries (d0ddb6… →0092b6…)
and validates their original proofs before the new allowlisted transition. The
cloud evidence registry and budget configuration remain byte-identical, retaining
the native child authorizations and original model request hashes. New operational
authorization is separate from the existing frozen evidence/model protocol.

## Validation and resume

Require offline tests, full private read-only replay, exact paid-claim accounting,
verified worker termination and committed policy/assets before any mutation. Only
the reviewed exact-owner release may be performed without policy adoption. Do not
activate collection or issue new paid calls in the recovery implementation task.
Actual adoption and LIVE_ENABLED=true require explicit operator approval. See
`reports/prospective_news_recovery_v1/report.md` and the deployment guide for exact
values, commands, exclusions, tests and any unvalidated behavior.
